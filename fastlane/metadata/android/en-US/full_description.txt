<p><i>Amethyst</i> brings the best social network to your Android phone. Just insert your Nostr
private key and start posting.</p>
<p><a href='https://nostr.com/' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>Nostr</a>
is an open protocol that is able to create a censorship-resistant global "social" network, transferring
notes and other stuff using relays. It doesn't rely on any trusted central server, hence it is resilient;
it is based on cryptographic keys and signatures, so it is tamperproof; it does not rely on P2P techniques,
therefore it works.</p>
<p><b>What you can do with Amethyst</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Post text, photos, videos, long-form articles, polls and voice notes to the relays you choose.</li>
<li>Follow people, reply, repost, react and quote.</li>
<li>Send private, end-to-end encrypted direct messages and join group chats.</li>
<li>Browse dedicated feeds: articles, pictures, short videos, live streams, podcasts, music, communities,
classifieds, calendars and more.</li>
<li>Send and receive Bitcoin Lightning zaps.</li>
<li>Track your training and share a workout &mdash; see below.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>My Fitness &mdash; track your training</b></p>
<p>Amethyst includes a personal training dashboard. Open the left drawer, tap <i>Workouts</i>, then
the chart icon in the top bar. With your permission it reads the workouts your watch or fitness app
has already saved to Android Health Connect (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin Connect,
Strava and others all write there) and shows you:</p>
<ul>
<li>How much you trained this week, and whether that is up or down on last week.</li>
<li>Your four-week weekly average for time, distance, steps and climb.</li>
<li>Where your time goes &mdash; running, cycling, walking, hiking, swimming, rowing, the gym.</li>
<li>Your best efforts: longest distance, longest workout, biggest climb, most steps, highest heart rate.</li>
<li>How many days you trained, and your current streak of consecutive active days.</li>
</ul>
<p>All of it is computed on your phone and shown to you. Nothing is uploaded to produce it, and you
never have to post anything to use it.</p>
<p><b>Sharing a workout (optional)</b></p>
<p>If you want to tell your followers about a particular session, tap <i>Share this workout</i> on any
row. Amethyst opens a post pre-filled with that workout's figures; you review it and decide whether to
publish. One workout at a time, always with your confirmation, never automatic.</p>
<p>The Health Connect integration is read-only. Amethyst never writes to Health Connect, never reads in
the background, never asks for your exercise route or location, and only ever looks at the last four
weeks. You can revoke access in Health Connect at any time. Full details are in the privacy policy at
<a href='https://github.com/vitorpamplona/amethyst/blob/main/PRIVACY.md' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>github.com/vitorpamplona/amethyst</a>.</p>
<p>Amethyst is free and open source (MIT). There is no Amethyst server and no Amethyst account: your
posts go straight to the Nostr relays you configure.</p>
