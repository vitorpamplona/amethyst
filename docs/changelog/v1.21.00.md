# v1.21.0: In progress

## Improvements and Bug fixes

- Compresses video with LightCompressor-enhanced 2.3.0: an HDR (HLG or PQ)
  video now stays 10-bit HDR when compressed with H.265 on a device whose
  encoder can keep it, and is tone mapped to proper SDR everywhere else,
  instead of 8-bit output with HDR tags. iPhone Dolby Vision recordings
  compress from their HEVC base layer instead of failing, and an encoder that
  stops responding fails the upload within seconds instead of hanging it.
- Tells you, under the H.265 switch of the upload dialog, whether a picked HDR
  video stays HDR or is converted to SDR, and why.
- Names the picked video's own resolution on the Android quality slider ("4K
  video → 2160p, 8 Mbps") instead of the 1080p example.

## Build & Documentation

- Bumps `LightCompressor-enhanced` to 2.3.0.
